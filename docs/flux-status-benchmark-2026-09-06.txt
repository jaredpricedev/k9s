Flux Status benchmark — 2026-09-06

Scope: pure Status() scans of 10,000 distinct HelmRelease objects; each iteration visits every release once.
Subtests use exactly 4 or 64 annotations per release (including a 1,664-byte last-applied value), generation 7, chartRef, and realistic Ready/Released conditions.
80% are Ready with acknowledged requests, 10% have a new unacknowledged request, and 10% have an active Reconciling condition after acknowledgement.
Fixture construction is excluded from measurement. ns/op, B/op and allocs/op cover all 10,000 releases; ns/release is also reported.
This is a local CPU/allocation microbenchmark, not an end-to-end UI, Kubernetes API, or controller-latency measurement. Shared-host scheduling affects elapsed time.

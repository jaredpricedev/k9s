# Observed daily workspace history

Open a saved workspace and explicitly refresh with `r`. `6`/Tab opens History alongside the current Daily queue, Inventory and Coverage. History shows the window start, unresolved prior findings, observed additions/clearances/replacements and collection gaps. There is no background history collection. Returning from supporting views keeps this window, tab, query and event selection. Reopening the workspace, changing its scope binding or restarting the process starts a new memory-only window; earlier history is unavailable.

The first positive finding is labeled **first observed**, not its true onset. A finding seen again is **persistent** with first/last positive source times. **Added** means it was first obtained after a complete preceding matching query. **Cleared** means the finding was absent from a complete query with the same captured context, GVR, namespace and workspace selector. This does not prove cause resolution or resource health. If a different UID appears at the name, the old identity is **replaced**. A denied, absent, unavailable, stale, canceled, omitted or truncated query retains prior positive evidence as **unknown**. Partial refreshes can update the current queue while History keeps unresolved findings separately.

Enter/`v` opens exact retained finding/query evidence. `i` opens a fresh investigation of the captured UID; it cannot silently substitute a replacement. Search is local and per-tab. `J` opens native Job/CronJob review with its current source identity checks, existing guarded trigger/suspend actions and scoped logs.

History also retains at most 64 typed Job/CronJob source snapshots from the kinds/labels already requested by this workspace. Schedule details reuse Job review's timezone/DST, suspend, deadline, concurrency and expected-match assumptions. Unselected Jobs/Pods are marked unqueried; child labels can differ. These records are not execution history, and missing retained Jobs alone never prove a missed run. Calculated future matches remain separate from observed Job conditions and source timestamps. Entries missing from the latest refresh stay explicitly unconfirmed, with their earlier capture time.

Finding history retains at most 512 changes and 2,000 tracked findings for 24 hours; pruning/caps are visible as omitted entries. TTL/history deletion, RBAC, bounded lists and explicit intervals leave gaps. Safe text projections retain exact resource identity and source times, exclude raw object specs/environment/Secret values and redact recognizable credentials. Model and native-cell checks cover complete versus partial refresh, stale/duplicate query evidence, replacements, retention, restart, schedule limits, supported widths and return state. They do not establish live-cluster behavior or operator usability measurements.
